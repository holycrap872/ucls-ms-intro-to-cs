## Essential Questions

- How do we use variables in games?
- How do we model complex interactions between sprites?

## Lesson Plan

In this lesson, students work by themselves to create a relatively simple game
that involves a bunch of "pop ups". The purpose is to get them back in the habit
of creating games via the game loop algorithm and to have them use variables in
a game-like environment for the first time. The reason that students work
individually is to identify students who have been overly dependent on their
partners throughout the previous lessons.
